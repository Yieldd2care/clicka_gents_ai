Common Questions

Questions, Answered Straight.

How long does a project take?

Most builds ship in 3–8 weeks depending on scope. Discovery and SOP work happens in the first 1–2 weeks. Build follows. We don't do six-month “transformation programs.”

Do I need to know what AI I want?

No. That's our job. You bring the business problem and the numbers behind it. We figure out whether it's an agent, an automation, a dashboard, or none of the above.

What does it cost?

Engagements start in the mid-four figures (USD) for focused builds and scale up for full systems. We share scoped pricing after the discovery call — no hourly games, no surprise invoices.

Will my team be able to use what we build?

Yes. We document everything, train your team, and hand over tools you can own and edit. No black boxes.

What if my industry isn't on your list?

The list is illustrative, not exhaustive. If your business runs on processes, AI can help. The discovery call is free — we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.
